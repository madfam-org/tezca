# Tezca Public Repo Sanitization Contract

Date: 2026-06-01
Status: launch-blocking when Tezca is used for active product delivery, discovery, scraping, enrichment, or campaign proof

## Position

Tezca can influence product/campaign delivery and discovery claims. Public repo sanitization must keep auth, scraping, setup, deployment, and strategy docs safe and commercially truthful.

## Current remediation posture

- Preliminary audit found no exact credential-signature matches in tracked current-tree files.
- No repo-level pass is granted until current-tree scan, history scan, public artifact review, and owner approval are recorded in Tulana.

## Launch-blocking checks

A Tezca-linked platform/SKU cannot pass Product/Offer GA public-repo sanitization until evidence confirms:

- Auth and deployment examples are placeholder-only.
- Scraping/enrichment docs do not expose internal targets, credentials, customer data, or prohibited operational procedures.
- Public strategy docs do not overstate GA readiness or campaign capability.
- Production domains and platform claims align with Tulana readiness evidence.
